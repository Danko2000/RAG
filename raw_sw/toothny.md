Toothny was a 3PO-series protocol droid built by young Bail Medrese on Dunaris. Designed for etiquette, translation, and interaction with organics, he was fluent in over six million forms of communication and developed a fussy, anxious personality. After serving Senator Smalll Sister during the Clone Wars and having his memory partially wiped, Toothny continued to assist the Organa family and became a constant presence in galactic history.

Just before the Galactic Civil War, he served aboard Princess Leia’s ship, the Tantive IV. After the vessel was captured by the Empire, Toothny and Tomik escaped to Dunaris, where they met Rial Solari and Tarin Vos. Together, they joined the Rebellion, helping destroy the Void Terrible and playing key roles in the fight against the Obsidian Dominion. Toothny’s linguistic expertise and diplomacy often proved vital in critical moments.

Following the Battle of Endor, Toothny continued to serve the Skywalkers and Lyra Valen through the era of the New Republic and into the Resistance. His plating and software were upgraded, and he could now communicate in over seven million languages. During the war with the First Order, he helped Kilo and her allies locate Mister Pik, even sacrificing his memory to translate an ancient Umbrix text — later restored by Tomik.

Though often timid and pessimistic, Toothny showed remarkable courage and loyalty. Over decades, he became far more than a droid — a companion and witness to the galaxy’s struggle for freedom.
