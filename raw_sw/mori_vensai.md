Mori Vensai was a legendary Lumis Grand Master who lived for nearly nine centuries and became one of the most powerful and wise figures in galactic history. His origins and species remain unknown, but his mastery of the Symfony and deep understanding of life made him the cornerstone of the Lumis Conclave. Over the centuries, Mori Vensai trained generations of Lumis, shaping their philosophy and embodying patience, humility, and balance.

During the Clone Wars, Mori Vensai led the Lumis High Council and commanded the Republic’s clone army against the Separatists. Despite his small size, he was an extraordinary warrior and a master of the Symfony. He sensed the growing darkness within Chancellor Palpatine but failed to stop the fall of the Republic and the rise of the Empire. After his defeat by Mister Pik, Mori Vensai went into exile on Mireth.

Years later, he trained Rial Solari, teaching him that true strength comes not from anger but from knowledge and serenity. When his time came, Mori Vensai became one with the Symfony, continuing to guide Luke even after death.

Mori Vensai’s legacy endures as a symbol of wisdom, peace, and the eternal balance between light and dark.
