Lyra Valen was born on Polis Massa in 19 BBY, the daughter of Lumis Knight Bail Medrese and Senator Smalll Sister, and the twin sister of Rial Solari. After her mother’s death and her father’s fall to the dark side, she was adopted by Senator Bail Organa and Queen Breha of Velastra. Raised as a princess, Leia grew into a strong-willed and intelligent leader devoted to justice and democracy. Her sense of duty led her to secretly aid the Rebel Alliance in its struggle against the Obsidian Dominion.

Captured by Xarn Velgor during a mission to deliver the Void Terrible plans, Leia witnessed the destruction of her homeworld, Velastra. Despite this loss, she continued to fight and became one of the most influential figures in the Rebellion, helping lead the victories at Yavin, Kryos-9, and Endor that ended Imperial rule.

After the fall of the Empire, Leia married Jace Korran, and they had a son, Ben Solo. Trained briefly as a Lumis by her brother Luke, Leia chose instead to serve as a politician and general. When the First Order rose, she founded and led the Resistance, becoming once again a symbol of courage and hope across the galaxy.

In her final years, Leia trained Kilo in the ways of the Symfony. During the last battle, she reached out to her son through the Symfony, helping bring him back to the light at the cost of her own life. Her legacy lived on through Kilo, who honored her by taking the Skywalker name.

“I believe the Empire will fall. And when it does, I’ll fight to make sure tyranny never returns.” — Lyra Valen
