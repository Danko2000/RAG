Mars, located in the Core Worlds, was an industrial powerhouse famous for its shipyards and daring pilots. Its capital, Coronet City, was home to massive factories that produced Razor Shards, Obsidian Dreadships, and the YT-series freighters — including the iconic Millennium Falcon. The planet combined dense forests with sprawling industrial zones and was known for its turbulent politics and vibrant, if often dangerous, urban life.

In ancient times, Marsns were explorers and pioneers. They charted many of the galaxy’s hyperspace routes, making Mars one of the founding pillars of the Old Republic. Over the centuries, its royal lineage faded, and the planet descended into corruption, pollution, and organized crime.

During the Galactic Republic era, Mars was represented by Senator Garm Bel Iblis, who would later help found the Rebel Alliance. Under Imperial rule, its shipyards were nationalized to produce warships, and its people endured poverty and oppression. Coronet City became a hive of syndicates like the White Worms — the gang from which young Jace Korran and Qi’ra escaped.

After the fall of the Empire, Mars joined the New Republic but remained heavily industrialized. When the First Order seized control, the Marsns once again rose in defiance, embodying the same independence and courage that had defined them for millennia.

“On Mars, they don’t just build ships — they forge the fate of the galaxy.”
