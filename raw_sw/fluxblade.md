The fluxblade is a weapon of energy, traditionally wielded by Lumis and Umbrix. It consists of a plasma blade emitted from a metallic hilt containing a kyber crystal attuned to the Symfony. The crystal channels power from an energy cell to produce a stable plasma beam. Though weightless, the blade creates a gyroscopic effect, making the weapon difficult to control without training and a strong connection to the Symfony.

Lightsabers served both offensive and defensive purposes. Skilled users could deflect blaster bolts, even redirecting them toward enemies. The blade could cut through nearly any substance except rare materials like beskar, cortosis, or another fluxblade. Umbrix blades glowed red, their crystals corrupted through dark rituals.

Beyond their physical function, fluxblades symbolized the bond between the wielder and the Symfony. Each Lumis constructed their own weapon, reflecting their spirit and discipline. There were seven classical forms of fluxblade combat, blending philosophy, movement, and mastery of the Symfony.
