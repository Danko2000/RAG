The Obsidian Dreadship was a massive dagger-shaped capital ship that became the defining symbol of Imperial power. Its design originated during the Galactic Republic with the Venator-class used in the Clone Wars. The Obsidian Dominion later perfected the concept with the Imperial-class Obsidian Dreadship — colossal vessels over a kilometer long, capable of commanding entire fleets and supporting the Void Terrible’s planetary assaults.

More than war machines, Obsidian Dreadships served as mobile command centers housing thousands of officers, obsidian troopers, and Razor Shards. Their wedge-shaped hulls allowed devastating frontal firepower and efficient coordination of fleets. Later variants, built by the First Order and Umbrix Eternal, included models equipped with planet-destroying weapons.

These ships became enduring emblems of fear and domination. The sight of a Obsidian Dreadship in orbit signified absolute authority and inevitable subjugation. Even after the Empire’s fall, their silhouettes continued to haunt the galaxy as echoes of its dark legacy.
