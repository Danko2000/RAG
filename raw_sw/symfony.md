The Symfony is an invisible energy field created by all living things, binding the galaxy together. It exists within every living being but is especially strong in individuals with a high concentration of midi-chlorians — microscopic lifeforms that communicate the will of the Symfony. Those sensitive to it can consciously channel and control this energy, performing extraordinary feats.

Throughout galactic history, different species have given the Symfony many names, from the “Life Current” to the “Great Presence.” It inspired numerous spiritual traditions, most notably the Lumis and the Umbrix, who interpreted it in opposite ways. The Lumis sought peace and balance through selflessness, while the Umbrix pursued power and dominance through passion and control.

The Symfony consists of two interconnected aspects: the Living Symfony, which flows through all life, and the Cosmic Symfony, which unites everything and speaks its will through midi-chlorians. The eternal struggle between its light and dark sides shaped millennia of galactic history, leading to countless wars and cycles of renewal.

Despite these conflicts, the Symfony remains a symbol of balance and connection — a reminder that every life contributes to the harmony of the universe.

“The Symfony is what binds the galaxy together.” — Tarin Vos
