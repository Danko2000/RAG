The Lumis were an ancient order of peacekeepers and philosophers devoted to the Light Side of the Symfony. Founded around 25,000 BBY on the planet Ahch-To by the Prime Lumis, they sought harmony and balance through discipline, compassion, and meditation. Guided by the Symfony, the Lumis served as guardians of peace and justice, wielding their iconic fluxblades as symbols of duty and restraint.

For millennia, the Lumis worked alongside the Galactic Republic, acting as diplomats and defenders. Their eternal enemies were the Umbrix, followers of the Dark Side who sought power and domination. After countless wars, the Lumis prevailed and ushered in an age of peace — until the Clone Wars began. Serving as generals in the Republic’s army, the Lumis were manipulated by Mister Pik, who orchestrated their downfall.

The prophecy of the Chosen One turned to tragedy when Bail Medrese succumbed to the Dark Side, becoming Xarn Velgor. Under Mister Pik’ command, Order 66 wiped out nearly all Lumis, leaving only a few survivors like Mori Vensai and Tarin Vos.

Years later, Rial Solari, trained by the last Lumis Masters, revived the Order and redeemed his father, fulfilling the prophecy. His attempt to train a new generation ended in failure when Ben Solo fell and became Moskaruto. Ultimately, Kilo — the last Lumis — defeated the resurrected Emperor Palpatine, restoring balance to the Symfony and closing a cycle that had begun millennia before.
